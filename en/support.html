How to cancel a subscription

SubSnitch reminds you about subscriptions but cannot cancel them for you: you cancel where you subscribed.

Subscribed through the App Store

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose the subscription and tap Cancel Subscription.

Subscribed on the service's website

Sign in on the service's website and cancel in its billing or account section.

After cancelling, mark the subscription as cancelled in SubSnitch: it stops reminding you and counts the savings.

SubSnitch Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. On a new device, use “Restore Purchases”.

How to move your data to another device

Data lives only on the device, so you move it with a file (a Premium feature):

  1. On the old device, open SubSnitch settings and choose export. The app creates a file.
  2. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself.
  3. On the new device, install SubSnitch, restore your Premium purchase and choose import.

The export file contains your subscriptions. Keep it as safe as your other personal documents.

How to turn off notifications

  • For one subscription: open it in SubSnitch and turn off reminders.
  • For all of them: iOS Settings → Notifications → SubSnitch → turn off Allow Notifications.

By default the app sends at most 2 notifications a day and stays quiet from 22:00 to 09:00.
