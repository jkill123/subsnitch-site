Privacy Policy

In short: everything you enter in SubSnitch stays on your device. We do not collect your data.

What the app stores

The subscriptions you add (name, amount, currency, period, dates) and your settings. All of it is stored only on your device, in the app's local database.

If you import subscriptions from a screenshot, the text is recognized on the device using built-in iOS technology (Apple Vision). The screenshot is not sent anywhere and is not kept by the app.

What the app does not have

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No server of our own: your subscriptions are never sent to us.
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics and no tracking.

When the app uses the network

Only to buy Premium, check its status and load exchange rates:

  • The app store (App Store) processes the payment under Apple's rules.
  • RevenueCat is a service that confirms the purchase. RevenueCat sees an anonymous identifier created by the app and the fact of the purchase. Your subscriptions, amounts and other app data are not sent to it.
  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) is an exchange-rate service. To show your spending in US dollars and in your local currency, the app asks it for current rates no more than once a day. The request contains only a currency code (USD): no data about you, your subscriptions or your device, no cookies and no identifiers. As with any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address; the app does not store it or pass it on. The rates are stored only on your phone. Without internet the app uses the last saved rates or the rates you entered by hand.

Notifications

Charge reminders are scheduled and shown on the device itself. No push server is used.

How to delete your data

Delete the app and all of its data is deleted with it. An export file, if you created one, stays wherever you saved it and is deleted by you.

Children

The app is not designed to collect data about anyone, including children, and does not collect it.
