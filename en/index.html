Your subscriptions under control. Your data stays with you.

SubSnitch is a private tracker for auto-renewing subscriptions. It reminds you before a charge, shows what you spend and how much you saved by cancelling.

On-device only

No accounts, no servers. Your subscriptions are stored on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Reminders before charges

A notification a few days before renewal, so you have time to keep or cancel.

Spending and savings

What you pay per month and per year, and how much you saved by cancelling.

No ads, no analytics

No advertising or analytics SDKs. The network is used only to buy Premium.

Free and Premium

Track up to 3 active subscriptions for free. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: unlimited subscriptions, import from an App Store subscriptions screenshot, and file export/import.

The app is in development. Languages: English, Ukrainian and Russian.
